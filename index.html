Grouped by date, earliest first. Hold cheques show in light red. Press Pay to record a cash or cheque payment. Fully paid cheques move to the Paid list below.

Paid cheques

Stopped cheques

Cheques whose payment was stopped at the bank. They are not counted in the balance to pay. Press Letter to make the bank letter again.

Recently updated cheques

1. Choose the cheques to stop

Click a cheque to add it to the letter.

Paid cheques are not listed. Press Mark selected as Stop to show the cheques as Stop on the dashboard. One letter is made for each payee (supplier), each on its own page.

2. Letter details

Saved in your database and used for every letter. Type the account number once, then press Save details.

3. Letter

Status options

These names appear in every Status dropdown: the dashboard rows, the Add cheque form and the filters.

The four built-in names cannot be changed because the totals and lists depend on them.

Add your own status

A status you add works like Pending and Hold: the cheque stays in the Cheque details list and its balance is still to pay.

Letter reasons

These names appear in the Reason dropdown of the Stop Letter tab (the letter says "due to the ...").

Supplier names

Supplier names have their own tab, where you can add, rename and delete them. The Supplier dropdown everywhere uses that list.

Cheque to print

Pick a cheque from the register, or type a new one. Then choose the bank and print on the real cheque leaf.
